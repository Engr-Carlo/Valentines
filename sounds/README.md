# Sound Effects

Put sound effect files here to add audio feedback to the website!

## Recommended sounds:

- **click.mp3** - Button click sound
- **success.mp3** - Success/achievement sound (for quiz, game completion)
- **pop.mp3** - Pop sound (for reveals, card flips)
- **whoosh.mp3** - Swoosh sound (for transitions)
- **heart.mp3** - Heart sound (for love interactions)
- **sparkle.mp3** - Magical sparkle sound
- **bell.mp3** - Celebration bell/chime

You can find free sound effects at:
- freesound.org
- zapsplat.com
- mixkit.co

Just download MP3 files and place them in this folder!
The website will automatically detect and use them.
